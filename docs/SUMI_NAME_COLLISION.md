## Nearby / different goods

- **sumi** (ink): common Japanese noun; expected lexical overlap, not a PDF product.
- **pdf-sumy**: 2015 summarizer library, different spelling and purpose.
- **npm `sumi`**: 2015 test package, unrelated.

## Recommendation

Use “Sumi PDF” as a working product name. If the project becomes commercial or widely public, commission a formal trademark search in relevant classes (software, SaaS) and confirm domain availability before printing the name on paid surfaces.
